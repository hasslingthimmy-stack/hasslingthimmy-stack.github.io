Aktierobot Paper Data Collector

About this application

Aktierobot Paper Data Collector is a private, non-commercial application under development for personal US stock market research and simulated paper trading analysis.

Purpose and permissions

The intended Alpaca integration requests data-only OAuth access using scope=data and env=paper.

The data collector is not intended to place trades or modify brokerage accounts.

Availability

This application is for private personal use. It is not offered as a public service.

This independent application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Alpaca.
